## About the App

Pendlerlogg is an iOS application that helps commuters in Norway ("pendlere") keep a record of overnight stays, home trips and receipts, and adds them up into totals for the commuter deduction on the Norwegian tax return.

## Not Tax Advice

**Pendlerlogg is a record-keeping tool. It is not tax advice.**

- The app does not decide whether you qualify for the commuter deduction. The qualifying criteria in the app are a checklist you tick off yourself.
- The rates and rules the app uses are based on information published by Skatteetaten (the Norwegian Tax Administration). They have not been checked by a tax professional, and they may be incomplete, out of date, or simplified. For example, the app counts nights away rather than days away.
- The totals the app shows are estimates. You are responsible for the information you submit on your tax return and for keeping the documentation Skatteetaten may ask for.

Check every figure against Skatteetaten's own guidance before you rely on it, and consult Skatteetaten or a tax professional if you are unsure.
