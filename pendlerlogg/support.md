# Pendlerlogg Support

Pendlerlogg logs your overnight stays, home trips and receipts as a Norwegian commuter (pendler), and adds them up into the numbers for the commuter deduction on your tax return.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is this tax advice?

No. Pendlerlogg is a record-keeping tool. It records and adds up what you enter, using Skatteetaten's published rates, but it does not decide whether you qualify for the commuter deduction. Check every number against Skatteetaten before you use it on your tax return.

### Why doesn't the summary show totals for a year?

Rates are bundled with the app, one set per tax year, and every value is checked by hand against Skatteetaten. Skatteetaten publishes a year's rates around December, and a new year's rates arrive in an app update after that. Until then, the summary shows no totals for that year and says why.

### How are the totals calculated?

The summary shows each total with the rate and the count it comes from, so you can check it by hand:

- **Lodging (losji):** the cost of your stays. A stay that crosses New Year is split by night between the two years.
- **Food (kost):** the number of nights for each type of accommodation, multiplied by that type's daily rate. Skatteetaten counts days away, while the app counts nights, since stays have no times. The summary says so.
- **Home visits (besøksreiser):** your one-way trips times your travel distance times the rate per km, capped at the yearly maximum, minus the yearly threshold (egenandel). Daily travel between your commuter home and work shares that threshold but isn't logged by the app, and toll and ferry costs are not included.

### Does the app check whether I qualify?

You tick off Skatteetaten's qualifying criteria yourself in Settings. The summary warns you about the ones you haven't ticked off. The app doesn't check them.

### How do I add a receipt?

Open a stay and scan a paper receipt with the camera, or pick a PDF or image from Files. You can also share a PDF or image to Pendlerlogg from another app, for example a hotel confirmation in Mail, and file it on a new or existing stay.

### Why does a stay show a warning?

A stay without a receipt shows a warning. The summary also lists documentation gaps, such as stays not paid by bank at a place where your lodging for the year is over Skatteetaten's limit for cash payments.

### How do I sync between my devices?

Sign in to iCloud with the same Apple Account on each device. Pendlerlogg then syncs your stays, home trips, receipts and settings through your private iCloud database. Settings shows whether sync is on. If you're not signed in to iCloud, your records stay on the device where you entered them.

### How do I get my data out for my tax return?

On the Export tab, export a year's log as a PDF or CSV file, together with a zip file of your receipts. Then save or send them wherever you like.

### Where is my data stored? Can you see it?

Your records are stored on your devices and, if you use iCloud, in your private iCloud database. The developer can't see any of it: the app has no servers, accounts, analytics or advertising. See the [Privacy Policy]({{ site.baseurl }}/pendlerlogg/privacy-policy).

### How do I delete my data?

Delete stays, home trips and receipts in the app. With iCloud sync on, a deletion applies on all your devices. Deleting the app removes its data from that device. To remove the copy in iCloud as well, delete the app's data in your iCloud storage settings.
