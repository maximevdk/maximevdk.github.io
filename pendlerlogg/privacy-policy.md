# Privacy Policy for Pendlerlogg

**Last updated:** November 4, 2026

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Pendlerlogg ("we", "our", or "the app") handles your information. Pendlerlogg is developed by Maxime Van den Kerkhof.

## Our Privacy Commitment

**Pendlerlogg does not collect any data.** The developer never receives anything you enter. The app has no accounts of its own, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code. Its only network use is Apple's iCloud, which keeps your records in sync between your own devices.

## Information the App Stores

The app stores the following on your devices, and in your private iCloud database if you are signed in to iCloud:

- **Stays:** dates, place, accommodation type, cost, whether you paid by bank, and notes
- **Home trips:** dates, direction, and notes
- **Receipts:** the scans, images and PDFs you add
- **Settings:** your commute start date, the names of your home and work locations, the travel distance you enter, and the qualifying criteria you have ticked off

## iCloud Sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, the app syncs your stays, home trips, receipts and settings through your private iCloud database (CloudKit), so the same records are on your iPhone, iPad and Mac. That database belongs to your Apple Account: the developer cannot see or access it. iCloud is provided by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

If you are not signed in to iCloud, your data stays on the device where you entered it. The app shows which of the two applies in its settings.

Like all app data, your records may also be included in your device backups (iCloud Backup or a backup to your computer), which are handled by Apple.

## Device Features the App Uses

### Camera
- **Purpose:** To scan paper receipts using Apple's document scanner, or, on the Mac, with a nearby iPhone through Continuity Camera
- **Storage:** Scanned receipts are attached to the stay you choose and stored like your other records

### Files and the Share Extension
- **Purpose:** To add a PDF or image as a receipt, either by picking a file or, on iPhone and iPad, by sharing it to Pendlerlogg from another app (for example, a hotel confirmation in Mail)
- **Storage:** The file is copied into the app and stored like your other records

## Exporting Your Data

You can export a year's log as a PDF or CSV file, together with a zip file of your receipts. The export is created on your device and only leaves it when you choose where to send or save it, in the share sheet or, on the Mac, in a folder you pick. What happens to it after that is governed by the app or service you choose.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your information to anyone. We never receive it in the first place.

## Deleting Your Data

You can delete individual stays, home trips and receipts in the app. With iCloud sync on, a deletion applies on all your devices. Deleting the app removes its data from that device; to remove the copy in iCloud as well, delete the app's data in your iCloud storage settings.

## Children's Privacy

Pendlerlogg is designed for adults who commute for work and does not knowingly collect information from children. The app collects no information from anyone.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
